# Strict Reviewer Checklist

Before coding / before presentation, answer each item with evidence.

## Research question
- What exact failure of vanilla HybridRAG are we targeting?
- What observation from Stage 1 proves this failure exists?
- What is our falsifiable hypothesis?

## Baselines
- VectorRAG under same corpus/prompt/LLM?
- GraphRAG under same corpus/prompt/LLM?
- Direct Hybrid under same corpus/prompt/LLM?

## Method
- Is the reranking formula explicit?
- Can every feature be justified?
- Is the candidate pool held constant in ablations?
- Are graph paths treated as evidence, not magically equated with reasoning?

## Data
- Are paper IDs stable?
- Are QA gold answers evidence-grounded?
- Are question types balanced?
- Is the benchmark audit retained as an artifact?

## Evaluation
- Retrieval recall?
- Evidence precision?
- Answer correctness judge definition?
- Citation validity/support?
- Latency?
- Per-question-type breakdown?
- Success AND failure cases?

## Claims
- Is every number generated by a script?
- Is every graph/table reproducible from CSV?
- Are limitations stated?
- Are we avoiding the words “significant improvement” without a statistical test?
